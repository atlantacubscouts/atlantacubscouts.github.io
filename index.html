Cub Scout Pack 586 is a Scouting America pack open to all families, proudly chartered by Neighborhood Church in Candler Park. We're a tight-knit, welcoming community of Atlanta families giving boys and girls an adventure-packed Scouting experience.

Candler Park · Atlanta, Georgia

A year packed with adventure

Here's what a year in Pack 586 looks like for your Scout.

⛺

Camping & Outdoors

Two campouts in the Fall, and two in the Spring. Always a highlight and memorable experience. Siblings & whole families welcome!

🚗

Pinewood Derby

Scouts design and build their own cars and race them down a 42 foot track at the Neighborhood Church on race day!

🌲

Outdoor Skills

Hiking, nature exploration, and hands-on learning in the field.

🤝

Community Service

Making a difference right here in Atlanta, alongside neighbors and friends.

🎖️

Rank Advancement

In supporting each scout's personal growth through education and skill achievement, their development is recognized through rank advancement, belt loops, patches, and more.

🛶

End-of-Year Kayak Trip

We close out every Scouting year together, on the water.

Join Pack 586

There are two steps to signing up.

1

Register with Scouting America

$85

An annual registration fee that officially registers your Scout with Scouting America. Click below to get going:

Register with Scouting America
2

Pay local pack dues

$150

Dues fund our local pack's activities: campouts, den meetings, Pinewood Derby, patches, belt loops and pins, Class-B t-shirts for new Scouts, and the end-of-year kayak trip.

Go to Neighborhood Church's giving page and:

  1. Enter $150 as the amount
  2. Choose “Give to BSA Pack 586”
  3. Choose “Give this One Time”
  4. Enter your payment method information
  5. Check the box to cover the processing fee
