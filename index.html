Welcome to Cub ScoutPack 586!

Cub Scout Pack 586 is a Scouting America pack open to all families, proudly chartered by Neighborhood Church in Candler Park.

We're a tight-knit, welcoming community of Atlanta families giving boys and girls (Kindergarten through 5th Grade) an adventure-packed Scouting experience. Our program includes:

  • ⛺ Camping & Outdoor Adventures

    Multiple campouts throughout the year

  • 🚗 Pinewood Derby

    One of our most beloved annual traditions!

  • 🌲 Outdoor Skills

    Hiking, nature exploration, and hands-on learning

  • 🤝 Community Service

    Making a difference right here in Atlanta

  • 🎖️ Rank Advancement

    From Lions (Kindergarten) through Arrow of Light (5th Grade)

There are 2 steps to signing up:

Step 1: Register with Scouting America

An $85 annual registration fee that officially registers your scout(s) with Scouting America. Click the link below to get going:

Regiser with Scouting America

Step 2: Local dues

Dues ($150) that fund our local pack's activities (campouts, den meetings, pinewood derby, patches/belts-loops/pins, class-B t-shirts for new scouts, and an end-of-year kayak trip)

Go to the Neighborhood Church's website: https://www.neighborhoodchurchatl.com/pack-586 and:
  1. Enter $150 as the amount
  2. Choose “Give to BSA Pack 586”
  3. Choose “Give this One Time
  4. Enter Payment method information
  5. Check the box to cover the processing fee.
